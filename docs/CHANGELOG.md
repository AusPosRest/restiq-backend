# Changelog

All notable changes to this project will be documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- **Hub rebuild keeps the offline clock** (#310): after a crash rebuild the 24-hour limit counts from the last real contact with the cloud (the newer of `bootstrap.json` and `outbox.ack`, whose times are kept through the rebuild), not from the rebuild.
- **Hub offline test matrix** (#310, HUB-BUILD-PLAN T1): `test/hub-matrix.e2e-spec.ts` runs the cloud app and the hub app in one process and covers online sync, 50 sales offline then drained, a crash between the cloud's commit and the hub's ack, two tills plus a KDS at once, the O1 offline lock, recovery after PGlite will not open (`openOrRebuild` + `reconcile`, then drained with no duplicates) and revocation with unsynced sales. `pnpm test:hub-matrix` runs it alone; it is part of `pnpm test:e2e` in CI. Shared harness `test/hub-cloud.ts`.
- **Hub open-sales resync after a rebuild** (#310): `openOrRebuild` sets `hub_meta.open_sales_resync`; once the outbox has drained the sync worker re-imports `bootstrap?include=open_sales` and clears it, so the open shift and open orders/bills acked before the crash come back. `GET /pos/v1/hub/status` adds `openSalesResync`.
- **Hub startup recovery** (#306, HUB-BUILD-PLAN H5): `src/hub/recovery.ts`, run by `hub-main.ts` before the hub listens. `openOrRebuild()`: when PGlite will not open, or opens without `hub_meta` while `outbox.log` exists, the data dir is moved to `<dataDir>.broken-<ms>`, a fresh store is opened, `outbox.log`/`outbox.ack`/`bootstrap.json` are copied in and `bootstrap.json` imported (offline). `reconcile()`: every journal op missing from `hub_outbox` is inserted (acked ones as acked, so the next seq stays right); unacked ones are first replayed locally through `applyOp` with their own ids and time (idempotent when the row is already there); then sales rows since the oldest unacked op with no op (orders, lines, bills created, bills finalized) go to the new `hub_orphans` table, never turned into ops; `GET /pos/v1/hub/rejections` lists them with `source: 'orphan'` and acknowledging one marks it on the till only. `SyncPushService.apply` is now the exported `applyOp(services, device, op)` (no cloud behaviour change). `compactJournal` keeps the last op (the seq high-water mark); `repairJournal` cuts a torn last line.
- **Hub status, O1 lock and Sync issues** (#307, HUB-BUILD-PLAN H6): hub-only `GET /pos/v1/hub/status` (any staff), `GET /pos/v1/hub/rejections` and `POST /pos/v1/hub/rejections/:opId/acknowledge` (`settle_bills`; acknowledge forwarded to the cloud when online, else queued in `hub_rejections.ack_pending` and sent by the next sync tick). `HubLockGuard`: after `last_sync_at + maxOfflineHours` new orders, counter orders, lines and combos answer 423 `offline_locked` (finalize, print and reads still work); a revoked device refuses every write (423 `device_revoked`). `hub-main.ts` listens on `parentPort` from the start (early device key held) and posts `{ type: 'status', ... }` after listen, worker start and every tick. `createHubApp(pg, dataDir, onOp?, hub?)`; `SyncWorker` `onTick` option and `forwardAcks()`.
- **Hub sync worker** (#304, HUB-BUILD-PLAN H4): `src/hub/sync-worker.ts` syncs the hub with the cloud every `syncIntervalSeconds` and right after each write - session status, first-run bootstrap with open sales, push of the outbox in batches of 200 (acks, local `hub_rejections`, stops at a deferred seq gap), pull (410 -> re-bootstrap, open sales only with an empty outbox), cached rejections, heartbeat; backoff 1-60 s while offline; revoked -> locked and staff PINs cleared. `src/hub/signer.ts` signs with the device's ed25519 key. `hub-main.ts` starts it when the Electron parent sends `{ type: 'device-key', pem, deviceId, cloudUrl, appVersion? }` over `process.parentPort` (`HUB_DEVICE_KEY_PEM` for tests only) and shuts down cleanly on `{ type: 'shutdown' }`/SIGTERM (worker, app, PGlite). `importBootstrap` loads `openSales`; `hub_meta` gains `online` and `clock_skew_seconds`; `createHubApp(pg, dataDir, onOp?)`; `verifyDeviceSignature` exported from the platform barrel.
- **Hub op recorder** (#300, HUB-BUILD-PLAN H3): on the hub every write is recorded as a sync op before its reply - `src/hub/op-recorder.interceptor.ts` serialises non-GET requests, builds the op from request + response (`src/hub/op-map.ts`, PINs never included), appends it fsynced to `HUB_DATA_DIR/outbox.log` (`src/hub/journal.ts`, plus the `outbox.ack` watermark and startup compaction) and inserts it into the hub-only `hub_outbox` table (`src/hub/outbox.ts`, seq from 1 with no gaps). Refunds and card payment intents answer 409 `offline_unsupported` on the hub. `createHubApp(pg, dataDir)` now takes the store and data dir. `SyncPushService` exported from the pos barrel. `order.line.update` carries `modifierPriceMinor` for new modifiers (required with `modifierIds`; `SyncLineUpdatePayload`), and the cloud's replay charges those prices instead of today's.
- **Hub LAN address and open-sales bootstrap** (#301, HUB-BUILD-PLAN B4 + B6): `PUT /sync/v1/hub-address` (hub only; 1-4 `http(s)` URLs with a private IPv4 or `.local` host and a port, else 400) and `GET /sync/v1/hub` (any keyed device in the outlet; 404 `no_hub`). Migration `20261114100000_device_lan_urls` adds `devices.lan_urls` and `lan_announced_at`. `GET /sync/v1/bootstrap?include=open_sales` adds `openSales`: the outlet's unclosed orders (plus paid orders with a queued ticket) with lines and modifiers, open bills with tenders, queued tickets with lines, the open shift with cash movements and the token counter (`OPEN_SALES_TABLES`, exported from `src/pos`; not in `CLOUD_TABLES`).
- **Hub bootstrap import and pull applier** (#296): `src/hub/import.ts` `importBootstrap()` loads the cloud's bootstrap snapshot into the hub's PGlite store (superuser + `session_replication_role = replica` for one transaction, fixed table list, rows the snapshot dropped are deleted) and saves it fsynced as `HUB_DATA_DIR/bootstrap.json`; `applyPull()` applies a pull page in version order and saves the cursor in the same transaction. Hub-only `hub_meta` table (`src/hub/meta.ts`). `CLOUD_TABLES` exported from the pos barrel.
- **More sync push op types** (#297, HUB-BUILD-PLAN B5a/B5b): `order.combo.add`, `order.line.void` (no PIN; audited `sync.void_approved_offline` with the approving manager), `order.transfer`, `order.discard`, `shift.open`, `shift.cash_movement`, `shift.close`, `clock.in`, `clock.out`, `ticket.bump` and `ticket.recall` (by `orderId` + `stationId`, since the hub cannot know cloud ticket ids). Each keeps the hub's ids and time; a lapsed permission is accepted and audited (S3). `addCombo`, `voidLine`, `transfer`, `discard`, the shift writes, `clockOut`, ticket `bump`/`recall` and `recordClockInIfNeeded` take an optional `replay`; online behaviour is unchanged. `Sync*Payload` classes are exported from `src/pos`. Refunds stay cloud-only.
- **Offline hub mode** (#293): `src/hub/hub-main.ts` runs the POS and kitchen controllers on an in-process PGlite store (all migrations, `restiq_app` role so RLS holds), listening on 127.0.0.1 behind a POS/KDS route allowlist (404 `not_on_hub` otherwise). `pnpm build:hub` builds `dist-hub/` (esbuild bundle + `argon2` and PGlite installed alongside, ~41 MB); `pnpm smoke:hub` boots it and signs in by PIN. New CI job `hub-smoke` (ubuntu + windows-latest). `setPrismaClient()` in `src/db/client.ts`.
- **Hub-ready sync API** (#292): bootstrap also sends the caller's own `tenants` row and its `brands`, and both now feed `sync_changes` (migration `20261113200000_sync_tenant_brand_feed`), so pull carries tenant changes. `bootstrap` and `push` answer 403 `not_hub` for a device whose role is not `hub`; `session-status` adds `isHub`. Pushed `order.line.add` keeps the line `note`. `sync_changes` is pruned daily to 30 days (not when `RESTIQ_HUB=1`), and pull with an older cursor answers 410 `cursor_expired`. New signed `POST /sync/v1/heartbeat` writes the same device health columns as the ops heartbeat, so `GET /ops/v1/sync-health` shows hub values.
- **Per-restaurant billing contracts** (#290): `GET`/`PUT /ops/v1/tenants/:id/subscription/contract` (`{ items, reason }`, audited `billing.contract_updated`, max 30 lines of description/quantity/unitAmountMinor per billing period/kind `recurring|one_time`). A save replaces every unbilled line; billed one-time lines are kept. With unbilled lines, `billing-link` checks out the recurring lines every period and the one-time lines on the first invoice (409 `no_recurring_items` without a recurring line), `checkout.session.completed` marks those one-time lines billed (`metadata.oneTimeItemIds`), and `cardBilling.amountMinor` is the contract's recurring total; no lines = the plan price as before. A save while card billing is active replaces the Stripe subscription's items (no proration) and puts new one-time lines on the next invoice; a Stripe failure answers 502 `stripe_sync_failed` with the contract still saved. Migration `20261113160000_billing_contracts`.
- **Top-selling items report** (#288): `GET /admin/v1/reports/top-items` and `/top-items/export` (CSV) over the payments filter (finalized bills, outlet, from/to). One row per item + portion (combos as their own row), quantity sold and sales = line totals incl. modifiers, before bill discount and refunds; highest quantity first. `listPayments`/`exportPaymentsCsv` now share `inFilteredBills()`.
- **Dev environment** (#280): Fly app `restiq-backend-dev` (`fly.dev.toml`, region iad, own Neon database and secrets, Stripe sandbox keys only) behind https://dev.niftypos.com.au. `pnpm stripe:check` verifies the Stripe keys in `.env` (refuses live keys; creates and cancels a test PaymentIntent).

### Changed
- **pnpm pinned to 10.34.4** (#276): `packageManager` in package.json, so the Docker image's corepack stops pulling the newest pnpm, which ignored the build-script allow-list and failed the Fly build.
- **Production can boot before Mailjet and an alert webhook exist** (#274): `MAIL_PROVIDER=simulator` is accepted (reset and invite mail goes to the ops dev inbox), and `ALERT_WEBHOOK_URL=off` means log-only alerts. `log` mail and an unset webhook still refuse to boot.
- **Two trusted proxy hops** (#272): `fly.toml` sets `TRUST_PROXY_HOPS=2`, because `api.dailypos.shop` now goes through Cloudflare and then Fly. `req.ip` stays the real client instead of a shared Cloudflare address.

### Added
- **Card readers (Stripe Terminal) and reader orders** (#286): operators register smart readers (S700 / WisePOS E) on the restaurant's connected account with `GET`/`POST /ops/v1/tenants/:id/card-readers` and `DELETE .../card-readers/:readerId` (`{ reason }`, deleted at Stripe too); an outlet's first reader needs `address` and creates its Stripe Terminal Location (`outlets.stripe_terminal_location_id`); `simulated-*` registration codes need a `sk_test_` key; 409 `provider_unavailable` without an active Stripe account. Reader list prices per country (`GET`/`PUT /ops/v1/reader-prices/:country/:model`, null = not for sale, audited). Owners see `GET /admin/v1/card-readers` (readers, own-country prices, `cardOnFile`, orders) and order with `POST /admin/v1/reader-orders`, charged at once by a one-off invoice on the platform customer with the subscription's card (`paid`, or `payment_failed` + Stripe's message; 409 `card_not_on_file` / `not_for_sale`). Operators list orders across tenants (`GET /ops/v1/reader-orders?status=`, `GET /ops/v1/tenants/:id/reader-orders`) and move them forward with `PATCH /ops/v1/reader-orders/:id` (paid -> ordered -> shipped -> delivered; cancelling a paid or ordered order refunds it). The platform webhook applies `invoice.paid`/`invoice.payment_failed` carrying `metadata.readerOrderId`. POS: `GET /pos/v1/outlets/:outletId/card-readers`; when a Live tenant with active card payments has readers at the bill's outlet, a `card_terminal` intent needs `readerId` (400 `reader_required`) and runs as a `card_present` PaymentIntent on the connected account (platform fee) sent to the reader with `processPaymentIntent`; `GET` syncs it from Stripe, cancel clears the reader and cancels the PaymentIntent, `simulate` taps Stripe's test cards on a simulated reader (test key only), and the Connect webhook settles it through `confirmIntent`. Intent views add `readerId` and `readerLabel`. Without readers (or in Test mode) the simulated terminal is unchanged. Migration `20261113140000_card_readers`.
- **Card payments on the guest's phone** (#284): `POST /guest/v1/bills/:id/card-payment` (`guestId` for a share, omitted for the whole bill) creates a Stripe PaymentIntent as a direct charge on the restaurant's connected account (`application_fee_amount` from the ops fee, `automatic_payment_methods`) plus a `stripe`/`card_online` payment intent row, reusing an open attempt with the same amount and cancelling a stale one. `POST /guest/v1/card-payments/:intentId/sync` and the new Connect webhook `POST /webhooks/stripe/connect` (signed with `STRIPE_CONNECT_WEBHOOK_SECRET`) share one settlement: `confirmIntent` writes the `card_online` tender, the share(s) are marked paid, and the bill completes like pay-all. A capture whose bill or share was already settled (or whose attempt was cancelled) gets no tender and is flagged `late_capture_refund_due`. Guest bill views gain `cardPayments` (Live tenant, active Stripe account, server keys, not a kiosk session) and `paymentsAvailable` counts it. A `card_online` credit note on a Stripe-paid bill refunds through Stripe first (502 `refund_failed` writes no note). Ops PATCH payments refuses `allowedMethods` without `card`. The terminal queue no longer lists Stripe intents. New env `STRIPE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY`, `STRIPE_CONNECT_WEBHOOK_SECRET`. Migration `20261113120000_stripe_card_payments` (enum value `stripe`, `tenant_payment_accounts.card_payments_active`, kept fresh by the ops Payments tab).
- **Stripe Connect and subscription card billing** (#282): restaurants get a Stripe Connect account (Accounts v2) from `GET`/`PATCH /ops/v1/tenants/:tenantId/payments` and `POST .../payments/onboarding-link` (status `not_connected`/`onboarding`/`restricted`/`active` read live from Stripe; allowed wallets and platform fee stored in `tenant_payment_accounts`; PATCH audited with a reason). `POST /ops/v1/tenants/:tenantId/subscription/billing-link` opens Stripe Checkout (mode=subscription, price from PlanPrice; annual = 12 months less the annual discount) and the subscription view gains `cardBilling`. `POST /webhooks/stripe` (raw body, signed with `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET`) applies checkout completion, paid/failed invoices (upserted by `invoices.stripe_invoice_id`; failure = arrears, never lifting a suspension) and subscription status changes. New env `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY`, `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET`; without a key the Stripe routes answer 503 `payments_not_configured`. Migration `20261113100000_stripe_connect_billing`.
- Per-tenant India CGST/SGST rounding (#244): `gstSplitRounding` (`exact_total` default, `equal_half_up`, `equal_half_even`) on `GET`/`PUT /admin/v1/tax-registration`, stored in `tenant_tax_registrations.gst_split_rounding` (migration `20261019100000_gst_split_rounding`). POS and guest bill responses add `gstSplitRounding` (null for AU, IGST and composition). Finalized bills keep their stored tax lines.

### Fixed
- **Void after fire on the hub** (#300): `voidLine` checks the manager PIN before its transaction; inside it the PIN lookup waited on PGlite's single connection and the void failed with a 500 after 5 s. Same checks and error codes online, except a wrong PIN on a line that can't be voided now answers 401 first. `finalize` with a discount above the threshold had the same deadlock: the PIN is now verified before its transaction and the result used only when needed (codes and order unchanged).
- **Hub store runs in UTC** (#296): PGlite took the host's offset on first open, so Prisma timestamps were hours off and imported prices were not yet effective on the hub menu.
- Guest QR/kiosk pay follows the tenant's Test/Live mode (#278): a Test-mode tenant can pay a share or the whole bill with `PAYMENTS_SIMULATOR` off; a Live tenant still gets 409 `provider_unavailable`. Guest bill views add `paymentsAvailable` so the web can show "pay at the counter" up front.
- Cash round-off is stored (#241, Q28): finalize also accepts tenders where the cash share is rounded per market rule (IN nearest Rs 1, AU nearest 5c; only the cash part of a split) and stores the adjustment as `bills.round_off_minor`. Cash tenders may carry `tenderedMinor` (cash handed over; change derived). Shift expected cash, the outlet payments list and the owner payments report now count what went into the drawer, so shift close no longer shows a false over/short. Bill, invoice and payments-report views expose `roundOffMinor`; tenders expose `tenderedMinor`/`changeMinor`; the payments CSV gains a trailing `round_off` column. Exact-total tenders still finalize as before.
- KDS Serve (expo) keeps a paid order on screen as Ready after its last ticket is bumped, for the Done tab's 12-hour window, so the runner can call the token. Refunded and discarded orders do not appear (#243, AP-cu-5).
- India CGST/SGST no longer over-collects a paisa: total GST is rounded once, then split CGST = lower half, SGST = remainder (Rs 289 at 5% is 14.45 = 7.22 + 7.23, was 14.46). Applies to bills, bill preview, invoices, refunds and reports, which all read the same tax result; IGST and AU GST unchanged (#244, AP-cu-15).
- Bill row locking under concurrency (#227): acquire `lockBill` row lock inside transaction for `confirmIntent`, `payShare`, and `payAll` to serialize concurrent payments, prevent duplicate tenders, and avoid unfinalizable tender mismatch states.
- Silent-device alerts run every minute without opening sync health, with one alert per silent period per API process. Recovery or a newer heartbeat permits another alert; timers stop on shutdown and are disabled in tests (#233).
- Production startup requires `ALERT_WEBHOOK_URL`; development and test environments may omit it (#233).
- #242 (AP-cu-1): till Lock/logout revokes only the presented POS token, keeping the same staff member’s kitchen and other device sessions active. Logged-out tokens cannot be replayed. Legacy tokens retain all-session logout; PIN and role changes still end every session.
- QR guests can add and edit special instructions (trimmed, up to 200 characters). Cart notes reach order lines and the kitchen; separate adds remain separate lines (#231).
- Cashier refunds can use an active staff PIN whose role has `refunds` and whose outlet assignments include the bill's outlet (empty assignments mean all outlets). Missing cashier approval remains 403 `forbidden`; an incorrect PIN remains 401 `invalid_credentials`. Managers still need a PIN. Credit notes and audit events retain the approving staff ID. No migration. (#240, AP-pe-2)
- Allow line edits after failed card attempts or with outstanding unpaid shares (#234 follow-up); retain protection for all tenders, active intents and paid shares.
- Refuse line removal, quantity or modifier price reductions, and manager voids after payment starts on an open bill (#234). Return `409 bill_has_payments`; additions and quantity increases remain allowed. Serialize the check and payment-intent creation on the bill row.
- Guest QR bill payment now sends any unsent staff-added lines to the kitchen through the shared finalize transaction. POS finalize uses the same hook once; already-ticketed lines are skipped, and rejected settlement creates no tickets (#228).
- An order left open for over 12 hours (the KDS live window, now one shared constant) no longer marks its table occupied on the POS table map or blocks a QR session there (#230). Nothing is auto-closed (Q26).
- Placing an order on a table whose open order is over 12 hours old (QR place-order or POS open table) moves that leftover order off the table (table cleared, status and lines unchanged, audited `order.detached_stale_table`) and places the new one. The leftover order stays in POS Open orders for End of day (#251). A table whose open order is under 12 hours old still answers 409 (#230).
